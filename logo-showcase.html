2. Anatomy & Geometric Construction

Mathematical Proportions
Grid Matrix: 32×32 units

The Archival Spine (Letter P)

Anchors the left vertical margin, mirroring a book spine or archival ledger. The upper semicircular arch represents the primary document volume and personal boundary.

The Knowledge Branches (Letter K)

Emerging precisely from the midpoint waist (y = 17.5), two 45° diagonal vectors branch outward to form the 'K'. They symbolize semantic hyperlinks, dynamic thought expansion, and note cross-linking.

The Vertex Node (Archival Ink)

At the exact convergence point sits a precision node rendered in vibrant Vermilion. It signifies an atomic note in the topology graph, an unshakeable point of intellectual synthesis.
